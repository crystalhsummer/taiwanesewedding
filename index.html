    跟岳父岳母說話Talking with her parents

    Before the wedding, call them 伯父 and 伯母, and use the polite 您 instead of 你. After the wedding, call them 爸 and 媽, just like your wife does. Her father is a doctor, so other guests may call him 醫生 (for example 林醫生). You still call him 伯父, then 爸.

    準備好了嗎？Before you fly

      Red envelope basics

      Even numbers

      Wedding 紅包 amounts are usually even. Amounts with 6 or 8 are popular.

      Skip the 4

      四 sounds like 死 (death), so avoid amounts like 4,000.

      You give some too

      The groom often brings 紅包 for the door games, the emcee, and helpers. Ask her family how many to prepare.

      New bills

      Use crisp new bills from the bank. It shows you prepared for a happy occasion.
